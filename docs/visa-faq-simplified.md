# China Visa FAQ - Simplified Guide

> **Quick Reference:** Essential questions and answers about China visa requirements  
> **Source:** Based on official China Consular Service Network information  
> **Updated:** December 2024

## 🔥 Most Common Questions

### 1. Which visa type do I need?

**Answer depends on your purpose:**
- **Tourism/Sightseeing:** L visa
- **Business meetings/conferences:** M visa  
- **Work in China:** Z visa
- **Study (over 6 months):** X1 visa
- **Study (under 6 months):** X2 visa
- **Visit family (Chinese citizens):** Q1/Q2 visa
- **Visit family (foreigners in China):** S1/S2 visa
- **Transit through China:** G visa or visa-free transit

### 2. Can I enter China without a visa?

**Yes, in these cases:**
- **Bilateral agreements:** Citizens of certain countries (varies by nationality)
- **Unilateral 30-day visa-free:** Citizens of specific countries for tourism/business
- **Transit visa-free:** 24/72/144 hours for transit passengers
- **Check your nationality:** Policies vary by country

### 3. How long can I stay?

**Duration depends on visa type:**
- **Tourist visa (L):** Usually 30 days per entry
- **Business visa (M):** 30-90 days depending on purpose
- **Work visa (Z):** 30 days initially, then apply for residence permit
- **Student visa (X1):** 30 days initially, then residence permit
- **Student visa (X2):** Up to 180 days
- **Visa-free:** Up to 30 days (varies by policy)

### 4. What happens if I overstay?

**Serious consequences:**
- **Fines:** Daily penalty fees
- **Deportation:** Forced removal from China
- **Entry bans:** Future visa restrictions
- **Prevention:** Apply for extension 7 days before expiry at local PSB

### 5. Passport validity requirements?

**Critical requirements:**
- **Minimum validity:** 6 months from entry date
- **Blank pages:** At least 2 blank pages for visa
- **Condition:** No damage or alterations
- **Renewal:** Must renew before visa application if insufficient validity

## ⚠️ Critical Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. **Working on tourist visa** → Deportation and ban
### 2. **Expired passport** → Entry refusal even with valid visa  
### 3. **Overstaying** → Fines and future visa restrictions
### 4. **Incomplete documents** → Application rejection
### 5. **Wrong visa type** → Activities not matching visa purpose

## 📋 Essential Documents (All Visa Types)

1. **Valid passport** (original + copy)
2. **Visa application form** (completed and signed)
3. **Passport photo** (recent, color, specific size)
4. **Supporting documents** (varies by visa type)

## 🚨 Common Rejection Reasons

1. **Incomplete documentation**
2. **Insufficient financial proof**
3. **Unclear travel purpose**
4. **Invalid passport**
5. **Previous violations**
6. **Security concerns**

## 🆘 Emergency Situations

### If visa application is rejected:
- **Review rejection reasons**
- **Complete missing documents**
- **Reapply after addressing issues**
- **Consider consulting visa agency**

### If passport is lost in China:
- **Report to local police immediately**
- **Contact your embassy/consulate**
- **Apply for emergency travel document**
- **Notify immigration authorities**
