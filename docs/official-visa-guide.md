## 🎯 Visa Types

### Work-Related Visas

| Visa Code | Visa Name | Applicable Personnel | Duration |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|----------|

## 🆓 Visa-Free Policies

### 1. Bilateral Agreement Visa-Free
- **Scope**: Based on bilateral agreements signed between China and relevant countries
- **Conditions**: Citizens of certain countries meeting specific conditions can enter China without a visa
- **Details**: Please refer to [China-Foreign Mutual Visa Exemption Agreement List](http://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zlbg/bgzl/lhqz/)

### 2. Unilateral Visa-Free Policy
- **Scope**: Citizens of certain countries holding ordinary passports
- **Duration**: Not exceeding 30 days
- **Applicable situations**: For business, tourism, family visits, exchanges, or transit
- **Details**: Please refer to [official link](http://cs.mfa.gov.cn/gyls/lsgz/fwxx/202411/t20241130_11535783.shtml)

### 3. Transit Visa-Free
- **24-hour transit visa-free**
- **72-hour transit visa-free**
- **144-hour transit visa-free**
- **Details**: Please refer to China National Immigration Administration website

## 📝 Application Requirements

### Basic Documents (All Visa Types)
- ✅ Valid passport original and photocopy
- ✅ Visa application form (fully completed and signed)
- ✅ Recent passport-sized color photo
- ✅ Relevant supporting documents

### Tourist Visa (L) Special Requirements
- ✅ Round-trip flight itinerary
- ✅ Hotel reservation confirmation
- ✅ Travel itinerary
- ✅ Proof of financial capability

### Business Visa (M) Special Requirements
- ✅ Invitation letter from Chinese enterprise
- ✅ Business license copy of the enterprise
- ✅ Dispatch letter from applicant's company
- ✅ Proof of financial capability

### Work Visa (Z) Special Requirements
- ✅ Foreign Worker Employment Permit Notice
- ✅ Invitation letter from Chinese employer
- ✅ Applicant's educational credentials
- ✅ Criminal background check certificate

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### 🔴 Critical Warnings
1. **Visa Validity Check**
   - Applicants should check visa validity and sufficient entry times before coming to China
   - Those with expired visas or insufficient entry times should reapply for visas

2. **Legal Compliance**
   - Foreigners in China must comply with the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China

3. **Stay Period Limitations**
   - Foreign citizens must not overstay in China
   - If extension is needed, apply to local public security organs 7 days before the stay period expires

### 🟡 Important Reminders
1. **Activity Restrictions**
   - Foreigners entering China must not engage in activities unrelated to the stated purpose when applying for their visa

2. **Restricted Areas**
   - Without approval, foreigners are not allowed to enter areas restricted to foreigners

### 🔵 General Reminders
1. **Item Declaration Requirements**
   - Foreigners bringing animals, plants, currency and other items into China must comply with relevant laws
   - Must comply with health quarantine, animal and plant quarantine, customs, and financial regulatory requirements

## 🚫 Entry Refusal Situations

The following situations will result in denied boarding, entry refusal, or fines:

1. ❌ **Coming to China without obtaining a visa in advance** (except those exempted from visa requirements)
2. ❌ **Coming to China with expired or invalid visas**
3. ❌ **Overstaying in China**
4. ❌ **Passport expiration during stay in China** (regardless of whether visa stay period is valid)
